Month stepper at the top of the Mes view. Device = Mobile | Desktop. The arrows are the only way to change month — there is no date picker here, because the app is built around one month at a time and jumping is rare. Prev is disabled in January and next in December: the store holds one calendar year. Touch targets are 44px on mobile and 32px on desktop. Desktop adds the "Agregar" popover, which carries the same three actions the FAB carries on mobile.
