The ledger card of an account page: every movement, in date order, and nothing else. IT EXISTS BECAUSE THE PAGE WAS BORROWING IncomeContainer, which is the Ingresos tab of the Mes view. That component opens with an icon and the title “Ingresos del mes” and closes with a converted monthly gross — two things a ledger has neither of. The account IS the title, and the number that matters is the running balance, not a monthly sum. IT HAS NO HEADER, and losing it is the point. The first build gave it one, and putting it on the real screen showed that four of its six items already sat in the AccountSummaryCard above: the account name, the edit affordance, “16% usado · Corte 4 · Pago 20”, and the current debt. Of the two that did not repeat, Alfredo cut both on 2026-09-01 and was right about each: · the movement count was noise on a list you can see; · Entradas and Salidas summed the ENTIRE ledger, every month of it, and sat directly under a chart labelled “Últimos 30 días”. Two different time scales on one screen, neither of them saying so. A figure whose period is invisible is worse than no figure, because it invites a comparison that does not hold. With the header gone BOTH AXES LOST THEIR REASON. Balance=Deuda|Saldo existed to switch the headline label and its colour; Device existed to hide the Entradas/Salidas pair on a phone. Neither has anything left to switch, so this is one component and not a set of four. A variant that changes nothing is a variant nobody maintains. Device now lives entirely in the rows, where it belongs. The opening balance is no longer a strip above the list: it is a ledger-itemrow in State=Opening, the LAST row, because the ledger runs newest first. That is what makes the card one table instead of a table with a lid — it takes the same rules, the same left rail and the same right edge as the movements it explains. Rows arrive through the Rows SLOT, which prefers ledger-itemrow and accepts nothing else. Each row draws its own bottom rule and the last one is switched off by hand: CSS has last:border-0 and Figma has no last-child selector, so that override must be re-applied whenever the number of rows changes. The sample figures reconcile, on purpose: 1.541.300 opening + 543.200 out − 800.000 in = 1.284.500, and the scheduled entry does not move the balance. Sample content that does not add up cannot be used to review anything.
