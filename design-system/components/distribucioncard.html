Where the gross income of the month went, as one bar. Not a chart component — it always shows the same four segments in the same order: Obligaciones · Provisiones · Gastos · Neto libre. Segments come from kpi/*/default, so a segment can never drift from the KPI card it corresponds to. Hovering one dims the rest to 40% and reveals its amount on the right. Segments with a zero amount are not rendered, so the bar can have between one and four.
