Choosing one currency among several, in card form. It is a radio, not a button: the choice is exclusive and the state lives in the group, not in the card. That is why it carries the system Radio inside instead of drawing its own mark. The flag is not decoration: in an app that shows two currencies at once, it is what gets recognised before anything is read. Its colours stay raw on purpose — they belong to a country, not to us — so it lives in brand-mark/flag, exempt from C1 (see design-system/docs/16-marks.md). Selected=True paints bg/brand-alpha-10 with a bg/brand border; State=Focused is the ring and never replaces the selection: you can be focused without having chosen, and both have to be visible at once.
