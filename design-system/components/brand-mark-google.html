Google mark, used on the sign-in button. Its four colours are Google’s, not ours: they are raw hexes ON PURPOSE and must never be bound to a token, because a token implies we are allowed to change them and we are not. They have no dark mode either — the mark is the same in both, which is also why it sits on Brand and not in the Icon Library. Exempt from C1 by CONFIG.foreignBrand; see design-system/docs/16-marks.md. The GitHub mark is the opposite case and is a normal glyph: monochrome, inherits currentColor.
