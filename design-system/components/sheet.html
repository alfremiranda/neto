The panel every form opens in. Device × Footer. Mobile slides up from the bottom with a drag handle and rounds its top corners. Desktop slides in from the right and rounds only the left side. Different patterns, not a reflow — that is why Device is a variant and not a breakpoint. The scrim behind it is surface/scrim, not part of this component. On mobile the sheet is clamped to the visual viewport so the keyboard never covers the fields.
