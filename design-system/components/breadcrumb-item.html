One crumb. Type=Link is navigable; Type=Current is the current page, marked with aria-current, and therefore only exists in State=Default — it does not react to the pointer and does not take focus. State documents the link states: Default, Hover (underline plus a darker foreground), Focus (a 2px ring drawn outside, over the 4px padding that exists precisely to house it). The 4px padding-x is not decoration: it is the box of the focus ring. Removing it makes the ring cut through the letters.
