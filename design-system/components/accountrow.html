One account in the onboarding list. The leading check is not a choice: it says the account is included, and it is always on — what varies is whether the user can take it out. Type=Fixed is an account the product includes and nobody can remove (Efectivo). Type=User is one the person added: it carries the brand wash, the primary border and the remove button. The difference in weight is deliberate — it marks what is theirs. Show badge turns on the currency chip; Show description the second line. Focused is the two-ring shadow, clipped behind the node so it does not tint the translucent fill (see 19-choice-rows.md).
