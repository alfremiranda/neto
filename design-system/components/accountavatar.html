An account’s identity mark: the tinted circle in account/{hue}/surface with the account-type glyph in account/{hue}/accent. This is the pairing the colour picker chooses, and the only place the surface token is used. The account TYPE stays a nested Icon Account swap rather than a variant axis. Twelve colours times four types would be 48 variants for two decisions that belong to different people: the colour is the user’s, the type is the account’s. Glyph contrast verified against its own tint in both modes — a filled glyph is a graphic object (WCAG 1.4.11), the rule the favourite star was got wrong on. Ordered by hue angle: orange 21° · amber 32 · lime 86 · green 142 · emerald 161 · teal 175 · sky 200 · blue 221 · indigo 243 · purple 271 · pink 333 · rose 347. The neighbours are the pairs that measure closest, which is what lets someone see they are two colours.
