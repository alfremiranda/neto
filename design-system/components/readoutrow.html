One line of a data readout: chip, label, figure. It only ever appears inside TooltipReadout, on bg/inverse. Divider draws the hairline ABOVE the row, so a group boundary belongs to the row that opens the group instead of being a blank item slipped between two rows. Dim is the muted state for a line that is present but not in play. Swatch hides the chip for a line that names no series — a total, a note. The chip is readout/swatch/*, deliberately the same value in Light and Dark: the surface under it flips, so a colour that followed the mode would land on the surface it was not chosen against. Measured on the Dark surface, every category colour fails 3:1 that way (1.44 to 2.77).
