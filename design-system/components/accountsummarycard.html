The header card of an account page: name, type and the headline balance. Type follows the account kinds (Bank Account · Cash · Credit Card · Savings) and Device switches the desktop and mobile layouts. 2026-08-24: the OPEN item is closed. The icon was a bare Icon Account painted with account-summary-card/icon/foreground — a fixed purple that could not follow the account. It is now an AccountAvatar (size=SM), the same carrier the account card and the colour picker use, so the account page shows the colour its owner chose. account-summary-card/icon/foreground has no consumer left.
